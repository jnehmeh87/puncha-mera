The ultimate tool for time tracking, project management, and invoicing.

Puncha Mera helps you to stay organized and get paid for your work. With our easy-to-use interface, you can track your time, manage your projects, and send invoices to your clients in just a few clicks.

Why Puncha Mera?

Puncha Mera is designed for freelancers and small businesses who need a simple yet powerful tool to manage their work. Unlike other time tracking apps, Puncha Mera is not bloated with features you'll never use. We focus on the essentials and provide a streamlined experience that lets you focus on what you do best.

Features

Time Tracking

Track your time with our easy-to-use timer. You can start, pause, and stop the timer as you work, and the app will automatically calculate the duration.

Project Management

Organize your work into projects. You can create projects, add members, and track the progress of each project.

Invoicing

Create and send invoices to your clients. You can customize your invoices with your own logo and branding, and the app will automatically calculate the total amount due.

Analytics

Get insights into your work with our analytics dashboard. See how much time you're spending on each project and identify areas for improvement.

Daily Earnings

Track your daily earnings and see how much you're making each day. This feature is great for staying motivated and on track with your financial goals.

Income Calculator

Calculate your potential income based on your hourly rate and the number of hours you work. This is a great tool for financial planning.
