Welcome to your new Command Center!

Puncha Mera uses a simple 3-tier hierarchy. Let's build your structural foundation.

1
The Workspace

This is your root Organization. Think of it as your Agency name or your main Freelance Studio.

You usually only need to create ONE of these! Everything else you do lives inside it!

2
The Clients

Once you have a Workspace, you add Client Contacts into it. These represent the real-world people or companies you do work for (e.g., "Acme Corp").

3
The Projects

Finally, you start Projects designed specifically for those Clients. This is what you actually track your time against (e.g., "Website Redesign").
